# Government services pack: license register (verified by fetch, 2026-09-10)

Same method as data/packs/cooking/LICENSES.md: the licence or copyright statement of every source was read from
the source itself on 2026-09-10, BEFORE any content was taken, with the pack's User-Agent
`tamil-lm-research (contact@timegravity.ai)` and under two requests per second, and is quoted here as found. The wiki licence comes from
the MediaWiki `siteinfo` rightsinfo API; every other statement comes from the site's own policy or terms page,
or from its footer when there is no policy page. Decision key: include = used in the pack; exclude = not used,
with the reason. Rule applied: reproduction must be permitted by the site itself. The standard Government of
India clause ("may be reproduced free of charge ... source must be prominently acknowledged") and GODL-India
count; a clause that conditions reproduction on prior permission by mail does NOT count until that permission
has been asked for and received; all rights reserved, an absent statement, an unreachable site or a page that is
not machine-readable means exclude and take nothing. The raw fetch of every policy page is cached under
data/raw/packs/govservices/pages/ with its fetch date and HTTP status.

Attribution requirement: every chunk carries its own `title`, `url` and `license`, so CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution
and share-alike, and the Government of India source-acknowledgement condition, can be honoured per answer at
serving time.

## Notes

- Three portals with real procedure text (Parivahan GS-6, e-Sevai GS-9, Vikaspedia GS-15) permit free reproduction
  only "after taking proper permission by sending a mail". They are excluded for that reason alone, and a written
  permission request to each is the single most valuable next step for this pack: it would add the learner's and
  permanent licence procedure, the e-Sevai certificate FAQ and fee list, and the Tamil-language e-governance section.
- GS-2 is the only official portal in the pack and it is English. Nothing was translated (`machine_translated` is
  `false` on every chunk), so a Tamil answer built on it has to be written by a person or by a later, separately
  reviewed translation pass.
- Share-alike: any redistribution of the Tamil Wikipedia chunks or of text derived from them stays under CC BY-SA 4.0,
  with attribution to the article title and URL that each chunk carries.
- Unreachable is not the same as refused: uidai.gov.in (GS-4), tnpds.gov.in (GS-5), incometaxindia.gov.in (GS-3) and
  india.gov.in (GS-14) should be rechecked on a later date before the next pack version.
