# Agriculture pack: license register (verified by fetch, 2026-09-10)

Same method as data/packs/cooking/LICENSES.md: the licence or copyright statement of every candidate
source was fetched FIRST, from the source itself (the MediaWiki `siteinfo` rightsinfo API for the wikis,
the footer, copyright or terms page for every other site), on 2026-09-10, with the User-Agent
`tamil-lm-research (contact@timegravity.ai)` at under two requests per second, and is quoted here as found.
The fetched pages are cached under data/raw/packs/agriculture/licence/. Decision key: include = used in the
pack; exclude = not used, with the reason. Nothing was taken from an excluded source.

Attribution requirement: every chunk in chunks_unscanned.jsonl carries its own `title`, `url` and `license`,
so the CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and share-alike terms can be honoured per answer at serving time, the same
rule as the cooking pack (decision 2026-09-09).

## Notes

- Every government and university source that a Tamil farmer would actually be pointed to (TNAU, ICAR, KVK,
  AGRISNET, PM-KISAN, PMFBY, Vikaspedia) is either all-rights-reserved or permission-on-request. None of them
  was used. The pack is therefore encyclopaedic (Wikipedia) plus one small Wikibooks book, and it says so in
  README.md under "Missing".
- Two of the excluded policies (AG-9 Vikaspedia, AG-14 PM-KISAN) would allow reuse after a written request.
  Asking is the cheapest way to improve this pack; nothing here presumes an answer.
- AG-3 is in English and is kept as English. Nothing in this pack was translated (`machine_translated` is
  `false` on every chunk). An English page was fetched only for an item with no Tamil chunk.
- Share-alike: any redistribution of chunks_unscanned.jsonl, chunks.jsonl or of text derived from them stays
  under CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution to the article or page title and URL that each chunk carries.
- Machine check on the build day: the quoted statement was searched for on each cached policy page;
  every quote was found on its cached page.
