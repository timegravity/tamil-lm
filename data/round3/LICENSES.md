## Decision 2026-09-08 (Vignesh): Wikipedia-derived SFT rows

Rows in the everyday-life slice (and any other SFT row) whose answer text is grounded on Tamil Wikipedia or Tamil Wikibooks passages are INCLUDED in round-3 training. They are documented in the data card as Wikipedia-derived under CC BY-SA 4.0 (attribution: article/page title and URL kept on each row), the serving stack keeps the per-answer attribution line, and these rows are never redistributed under any other license.
